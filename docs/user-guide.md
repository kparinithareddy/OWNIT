# OWNIT - Comprehensive User Guide

> **Tagline**: *"Own More. Worry Less."*  
> **Target Audience**: End Users, Evaluators, and App Testers

---

## 1. Introduction to OWNIT

Welcome to **OWNIT**, your personal Smart Product Lifecycle Assistant! OWNIT helps you organize all your purchases, invoices, warranty cards, return deadlines, maintenance tasks, and repair records in one secure, accessible place.

---

## 2. Navigating the Interface

OWNIT provides a clean, responsive navigation bar at the top of the screen:
- **Vault (Products)**: View all your registered products, search, and filter by category or warranty status.
- **Scan Receipt**: Upload digital bills or snap a photo of paper receipts for automated parsing.
- **Warranties**: Monitor all active, expiring, and expired warranties with color-coded badges.
- **Timeline**: View chronological milestones from purchase to warranty expiration across all devices.
- **Notifications**: Check countdown reminders for upcoming warranty expirations.
- **Language Selector**: Switch instantly between **English**, **Hindi (हिंदी)**, and **Telugu (తెలుగు)**.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step User Workflows

### 3.1 Scanning a Receipt
1. Click **"Scan Receipt"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Drag and drop your store invoice (PDF, JPG, or PNG) into the upload zone, or click to browse.
3. OWNIT's OCR engine will analyze the receipt and display a list of detected products, prices, dates, and sellers.
4. Review the details, make any corrections if needed, and click **"Save Products to Vault"**.

### 3.2 Adding & Tracking Warranties
1. Navigate to any product's details page.
2. Click **"Add Warranty Component"**.
3. Select the coverage type:
   - **Comprehensive Warranty** (Standard overall product warranty)
   - **Panel Warranty** (Display screen coverage)
   - **Compressor / Motor Warranty** (Major component coverage)
   - **Extended Warranty / Screen Protection**
4. Enter the duration (e.g., `12 Months` or `2 Years`) and start date. OWNIT automatically calculates the exact expiration date and sets up milestone reminders!

### 3.3 Asking Your Product AI Assistant
1. Open any product from your vault.
2. Click on the **"AI Assistant"** tab.
3. Ask questions in plain English, Hindi, or Telugu:
   - *"Is screen flickering covered under my warranty?"*
   - *"When does my return window close?"*
   - *"What customer support number should I call for repairs?"*
4. The AI responds with verified information and clickable citations linking to your uploaded documents.

### 3.4 Preparing a Warranty Claim Dossier
1. If your device experiences a defect, navigate to the product page and click **"Claim Assistant"**.
2. Select or describe the symptom (e.g., *"Horizontal lines on display"*).
3. The Claim Assistant will:
   - Check coverage likelihood (`Confirmed`, `Likely`, `Unclear`, `Excluded`).
   - Check if your purchase invoice and warranty card are present in your vault.
   - Generate a professional, pre-filled **Draft Support Email / Letter**.
   - Provide an authorized customer support phone number and a confirmation checklist.
4. Copy the draft letter and submit it directly through the manufacturer's official support channel!

### 3.5 Logging Maintenance & Service History
1. **Preventive Care**: Check the **"Maintenance"** tab for category-specific care recommendations (e.g., cleaning AC filters every 3 months).
2. **Service Records**: When you visit an authorized repair center, click **"Add Service Record"** to record the date, technician notes, Job Sheet number, and repair cost.

### 3.6 Checking Safety & Recall Alerts
1. OWNIT continuously monitors registered devices against published manufacturer recall programs.
2. If an advisory matches your device (e.g., specific MacBook battery replacement batches), a warning banner appears on your dashboard with the official manufacturer verification link.
